## The ask — 22

Three doors at line 300 after *"nothing above was held back — every role, every
mark, every receipt"*, four counters printed inline, and the best single
formulation of what yes means in any variant: *"Signing in tonight moves none of
it: this register stores nothing on its own side yet. It is the only way to say
that a daybook should outlive a browser."* Two things hold it down. The ask
prints **"Your daybook holds 1 nights and 0 kept roles"** — a plural fault, on
screen, in the ask's own sentence, in the default edition; and NOTES claims this
exact bug is fixed ("`1 nights` is gone"), which makes it the round's predicted
believed-shipped-but-not-rendered finding. Small in pixels; located at the one
moment the page asks to be trusted.

## Inside — 21.5

- **The two hands are the round's sharpest doctrine**, stated and enforced:
  *"what the page watched you do, in grey, and what you told it happened
  somewhere else, in red. It never turns one into the other."* An `open` is
  witnessed and inkless; `applied` cannot be produced by a click handler. The
  daybook is a dated, append-only ledger that pushes the register down — the
  reader's half of the same document — and across a seeded week it reads
  exactly like a week: read-nights, a keep on Jul 29, an application on Aug 1.
- **The follow-up is the most product-shaped invention of the round.** Seeded an
  open on Aug 2; on Aug 4 the daybook asks, once: *"On Aug 2, 2026 you opened
  Sr. Product Manager · 6Sense. Did you apply?"* — Yes / Don't ask about this
  one; never the same night, dead after 21 days. It closes the loop between
  browsing and stating without ever inferring, and no other variant has
  anything like it.
- **The corrected numbers are design, not errata.** r02-c's 4,662 is replaced by
  the counted 4,781, the 119 double-marks are printed, and the bar draws "the
  split that does" add up. On a page where numbers are the prose, printing the
  overlap is the fix.
- The kept view bands by the reader's own keep-nights and prints the departed
  ClickHouse keep struck through, with the goneNote saying exactly what the
  page is entitled to.

What costs it: **the morning-after keeps the workbench and loses the register.**
Forced v11: the banner is right ("The register did not open tonight… no number
in it is current"), the kept roles print in full and stay climbable — *"What you
did is yours, and it does not depend on this register opening tonight"* is a
good sentence — but the page is 1,700px tall and the 6,423 lines do not print,
because `d.sheet` stores only summary figures. The 11pm reader who kept nothing
meets numbers and an apology. Note 09's *"It prints the last sheet it did
render instead"* promises more than ships. And the nights strip at true scale
is, by its own NOTES, "honest and nearly unreadable" — six of seven columns
within two pixels of the floor; I could not read my seeded week off it without
the note.

## Originality — 17.5

Two genuine inventions (two hands, the follow-up) — more new mechanism than
either competitor added this round — mounted on inherited r02-c machinery, with
the round's shared conventions (three doors, ladder, honest offer, kept copy)
adopted wholesale. Its own NOTES names the deepest problem: `applied` holds a
posting, and the product this register serves is a referral into a *company*.
The wildcard found the right questions; the page underneath them is the least
transformed of the three.
