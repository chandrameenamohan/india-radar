## The ask — 23.5

stops here … **300** lines are printed above. **6,123** more are set and not
printed" arrives after real value, and the offer is finally the true one: *"the

## Inside — 23

The system now runs through time, and it composes:

- **The verb is staged correctly.** `I APPLIED` appears only once a corner is
  folded; `a` does nothing on an unfolded line. After marking an answer:
  *"You marked an answer on Aug 4, 2026. What it said is yours; this page does
  not ask."*
- **The best sentence of the round**, verified on the running page against a
  seeded Aug 1 application: *"You applied on Aug 1, 2026 and have marked no
  answer since. Whether one came, this page cannot know: 3 nights of nothing is
  not a no. It is the same silence this sheet prints as nothing at all."* The
  register's silence doctrine, turned on the reader's own week.
- **The morning-after is the strongest of the three.** Forced v11: masthead
  becomes a boxed `KEPT COPY · AUG 4, 2026`, an ink-ruled (not magenta — the
  builder's reasoning is right) panel names v10 vs v11, the full 6,423-line copy
  prints, and the diff and fold-departure claims are withheld with the reason
  stated: *"whether they are still on a board is a question only tonight's
  register could answer."* The depth stamps carry the mark every hundred lines,
  so a reader at depth cannot mistake the copy for tonight — B suppresses
  claims; A marks the artifact itself. That is the more complete answer.
- **Visit seven greets honestly**: *"The register has not been resurveyed since
  you were last here. This is the same sheet"* · *"1 of your folded corners are
  no longer on a board"* (grammar slip at n=1) · *"Opened on 7 of the 8 nights
  since Jul 28, 2026."* YOUR NIGHTS renders mostly blank, which is the design's
  argument working.
- ja edition: SOV throughout, the dead end explains itself and prices one chip

What holds it at 23: **the date column changes hands** — a kept-then-applied row

## Originality — 18

The extension of the overprint rule to time — *"a history is the one kind of
data where most cells are empty and the emptiness is the finding"* — is a real
idea argued all the way down, and killing the inherited counter on purpose is
the convergence warning taken seriously. Against that: this is the third round
of one metaphor, and the round's core mechanism — the kept → applied → answered
ladder, dated, take-backable, burnable — is now structurally identical in all
three variants. The lane question ("can the overprint hold a reader's history")
is answered yes; it is no longer a surprising yes.
