## The four signals

1. **WHAT / FOR WHOM / WHY THEM** — labelled rows on the collapsed card, and
   then — the departure — the *rows of the comparison plate itself*. The
   founder said the card never says what the company is; this page made
   what-they-do / for-whom / why-them the organizing rows of the decision the
   reader is actually making. Strongest possible structural answer to Signal 1.
   Provenance said once in the aside (the brief's letter, followed best), plus
   a per-company SUMMARY row on the plate ("Written by this page from their own
   site, **not re-checked**") — though the collapsed cards themselves carry no
   per-card checked/unchecked distinction, which a and b both print.
2. **Status** — "Public, per Y Combinator" inline, in ink, structurally
   *incapable* of being red: "A status word cannot go red here, because red is
   not available to facts." Airbnb reads as a fact with its batch. Clean pass,
   and the strongest possible enforcement mechanism.
3. **The target** — 65.5.
4. **The baseline** — token-identical craft (the NOTES table is accurate; I
   checked the values), and the plate with two kept companies is the most
   *live-site* artifact any round has produced: a labelled-rail table with big
   tabular numerals about the reader's own decision. But the cold fold carries
   **no red at all** — the register's identity mark enters only with the
   reader's record. That is the round's best sentence made structural

   to the live site's red interpunct and platestamp, this fold shows the least
   of the baseline's energy of the three. The founder meets the restraint
