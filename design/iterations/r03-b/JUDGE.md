## The ask — 24

The most honest ask this harness has produced, and the honesty *is* the pitch:

> *"What signing in buys, exactly: nothing yet. … The button is a count of
> whether anybody wants it to go somewhere. That is the whole offer, and we
> would rather say so than imply a sync we have not written."*

Three doors, the third permanently silencing the ask, the counter rewritten as a
sentence in the page's own voice with the count's location stated ("four numbers
in your own storage: the bookplate at the foot prints it, and offers to burn
it"), and *"This is not a wall"* backed by a searchable sentence and a linked
data file. The seam arrives at 240 rows, after genuine value. Round 2's greeting
overclaim is fixed at the source (`seenSince` requires confirmed) and the
remainder is now *printed as what it is* rather than dropped — the honest
version of the sentence that was wrong.

## Inside — 23.5

- **The sentence conjugated, and the grammar holds.** The noun slot now offers
  *the 2 roles I kept* · *the one I applied to*, singular-aware, and selecting
  a tense rewrites the whole line: *"Show me the one I applied to … — what I
  did last, first."* Under the section head: *"These three words are yours. The
  register did not watch you and could not have known."* In ja the sentence
  stays SOV with the typed word inline. Keeping and applying are two keypresses
  (`x`, `a`), and `a` keeps first if it has to — the page not insisting on its
  data model in front of somebody telling it something.
- **The second week has a literal story**, in the page's native idiom, prose:
  *"You have read this register on 7 nights … On 1 of them you kept something —
  2 roles in all. You have applied to 1 of those, and nobody has answered yet.
  Your oldest unanswered application was 6 nights ago. All of that is your own
  record, kept in this browser. The register was told none of it."* That last
  number is the one a person actually comes back for, and *longest waiting
  first* is the sort they come back with. This is gap 1 and gap 3 answering
  each other.
- **The morning-after is disciplined.** Forced v11: `READ ON AUGUST 4, 2026 ·
  A COPY` in the letterhead, the full register printed from a byte-for-byte
  Cache copy, and — verified, not believed — **zero** NEW badges, no diff, and
  the deictic *Tonight* band converted to *First seen August 4, 2026*. A copy
  that converts its own tenses to dates is a copy that has thought about what a
  claim is. The kept shelf still works inside the copy. The Cache-not-
  localStorage reasoning (UTF-16 doubling, no re-serialisation) is the round's
  best piece of quiet engineering, and `?stale=1` ships because "a fallback
  nobody can see is a fallback nobody has checked."
- The greet's confirmed-only diff carries the departure line into the verb where
  it hurts: *"1 of the roles you kept is no longer on any board we read
  tonight."*

Held from 24 by its own named weakness, which is real on the running page: on a
returning night the greet plus the log put nine dense lines between the sentence
and the register, and the verb is undiscoverable from the register at large — a
reader who never opens a row will never meet `a`.

## Originality — 18

The conjugating sentence remains the only mechanism of its kind in nine
variants, and this round it grew grammar rather than polish — which is exactly
what the brief feared a third draft would not do. Dashed marks for reader-stated
facts against solid for board-read facts is typography making an epistemic
argument. But the surrounding mechanism set — three doors, ladder, nights strip,
kept copy, honest offer — is now the shared property of all three variants, and
B's claim to originality rests on one (excellent, twice-winning) idea now three
drafts old.
