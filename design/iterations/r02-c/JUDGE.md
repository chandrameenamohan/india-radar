## Originality — 17

A three-mark vocabulary that never grows, with absence as the fourth state
drawn at corpus scale; distance-as-answer; the zero; a 97-key chrome (42%
smaller translation surface than r01-c) with no orphaned English found by its
own sweep. Lane C's constraint is genuinely met, not worn. But the passport
metaphor is mostly a name — a stamp-grid cell and rail page-numbers — and
between its inventions the page is a stats header over a table. The inventions
are controls; the connective tissue is default.

## The contested number, settled

The strip prints **○ 1,009 · ▮ 543 · ⊠ 209 · (none) 4,662**, and
1,009 + 543 + 209 + 4,662 = 6,423 — a partition. But the marks are not
disjoint: **the rows themselves double-mark**. n8n's "Sr Growth Engineer
(Fullstack TS/Vue/NodeJS)" carries ○ and ▮ together, and the corpus has 119
such roles (83 remote∧open, 36 remote∧stated-closed; ⊠'s 209 is already
closed-minus-open, 213−4). So "no mark" computed by subtraction double-counts
the overlap: the true number of rows carrying no mark, under C's own
definitions, is **4,781** — exactly the corpus's not-remote-with-both-facts-
unknown. The printed 4,662 is short by 119.

Worse for the thesis than for the arithmetic: footnote 02 also claims "**93%**
of the ones we measured carry no mark", which matches the share silent on
*visa* alone (5,974∕6,423), not the share carrying no mark (4,781∕6,423 =
74%). On a page whose argument is that numbers can replace prose, the two
load-bearing silence numbers are both wrong. This is the text-light bet
losing exactly where it was placed. (Recorded, not double-punished: it is one
wound, counted once, under Inside.)

Also for the record: C counts 11 non-Latin titles where A counts 10 — the
Han-only classification edge both builders name; neither is dishonest, they
derive differently.
