## The ask — 8

The bound-in reply card is the best *written* ask of the round. "This copy is
the abridged binding… it exists to be counted… and it is not a trick" — and it
keeps its word: I clicked *Print the rest of this copy anyway* and the register
went from 3,955px to 8,225px with my scroll position intact, `ra.ask.answer:
"printed"`, `ra.bypass: 1` in localStorage, and the bookplate at the foot
updated from "shown 1 time, unanswered." The bookplate itself — every byte the
page keeps, printed, with *Forget this copy* beside it — is the most complete
trust move in any variant. The card not appearing when a filtered register fits
("nine results is an answer") is a decision the other two didn't think to make.

What costs it: **the first impression is 40 lines of Databricks.** The canonical
sort front-loads the largest company, so the signed-out reader's free sample —
the one moment the brief says must prove the register is honest — is one
company, bound short (40 of its 295). The builder knows (weakness #5). It
recovers the instant the reader turns to their own country's plate (Plate 03
gave me SumUp's real GB roles; search gave 40 real lines of 2,270 "engineer"
matches), but the reader the brief names — anxious, 11pm, first visit — sees a
wall of one American company's sales org before they see anything that looks
like *their* job.

## Inside — 8

The apparatus is the deepest of the three. The KEY TO THE SHEET is a real
legend ("a mark with nothing behind it is not printed at all"); quire signature
marks every 32 lines run A–Z skipping J, U, W — an actual compositor's
convention, doing actual wayfinding work. The VERIFIED · BOARD READ stamp on
each gazetteer entry, updating honestly under scope ("185 on the board · 56 in
this scope"), is provenance as furniture. Plate turns work from the keyboard,
the running head always answers "where am I," and typing into search is
debounced where the incumbent stutters.

But the register is more admirable than inhabitable. Role titles are bare
external links — there is no in-page role reveal at all, so the brief's "a role
reveals itself without a page turn" flow simply doesn't exist here; the
three-column printed spread is handsome and utterly uniform for thousands of
lines; and `j` from deep in the register warps the viewport back to the cursor
at the top rather than picking up where my eye was. The traverse bar — the one
place the abridged binding is visible as a proportion — is a 3px speck, as the
builder admits.

## Originality — 7

A's bet is stated in its own notes: the incumbent's language is good and only
half-applied. That bet is executed with real invention — the reply card, the
bookplate-as-privacy-panel, the quires — but the aesthetic, the plate chart,
the filter bank (the same nine selects as `site/index.html`, nearly verbatim),
and the register itself are the incumbent's. Held next to B and C, A is the
variant you could mistake for the page it was handed. Every mark is a decision;
few of them are departures. In a round whose scarce resource is evidence of a
different answer, deepening the existing answer earns a 7, not more.
