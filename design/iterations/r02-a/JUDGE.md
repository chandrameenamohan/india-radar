## The ask — 23

The best-instrumented ask of the round, and the most scrupulous.

- The free 300 are **dealt** — newest night first, one line per company round
  the table — so the first screen is Halter (NZ), Heidi Health (AU), Neo4j (IN),
  Netskope (AU), Reflection AI (GB). r01-a's forty lines of Databricks are gone,
  and the rule is printed on the finder line rather than left implicit.
- "This copy stops here" states the remainder in numbers and offers **three
  doors: SIGN IN · PRINT THE REST ANYWAY · NO THANK YOU**, with the counter
  under them (`asked 1 · signed in 0 · printed anyway 0 · declined 0`). Making
  the bypass a labelled, counted button beside the sign-in is the strongest
  single trust gesture in the round.
- The ask suppresses itself when a filtered view already fits (set the
  standpoint, press PRINT ONLY THE OVERPRINT: 83 rows, no card, footer directly)
  and — per the notes, and consistent with what I saw with the counter — is
  withheld entirely rather than flashed when Clerk has not answered.
- The bookplate ("WHAT THIS COPY KNOWS ABOUT YOU") prints every key with its
  value and BURN ALL OF IT; it even counts standpoint changes (`ra2.standpoint.set`)
  and searches (`ra2.find`) — r01-b's instrumentation idea generalized.

The half point of air above r01-b's 22.5, not more, because A answers the
hollow-gate gap by **refusing to fill it**: "carrying them would need a server
this register does not have yet." True, admirable, and the least motivating of
the three answers — "yes" still buys nothing a reader can hold today, and the
counter measures desire for a hypothetical the copy itself calls hypothetical.

## Inside — 22

- **The evidence sheet is the fullest of the three.** Credit Analyst — Monzo
  opens in place: VISA SPONSORSHIP *the posting says yes* in magenta, HIRING
  FROM ABROAD *the board did not say* in grey italic — silence given words
  exactly once, at the question the reader came with — plus board read date and
  ATS, ROUND *round letter not stated*, LISTED BY *its funding directory's
  growth-stage label*, UK REGISTRATION 09446231 · MONZO BANK LIMITED, the
  machine-written lines marked as machine-written, and a paragraph naming every
  derivation.
- **Folds are literal dog-ears**: every row carries a faint corner at its right
  edge; folding fills it magenta and washes the row. And folds are the one
  place the register can honestly report a departure — a fold whose URL left
  the corpus prints struck through. The build keeps no departure ledger; the
  reader's pins are one.
- **The cursor follows the eye** — scrolled to 6,000px, blurred the finder,
  pressed `j`: the first row in view took the cursor, scrollY unmoved. Gap 4
  closed and felt.
- The **return banner** is the only honest one of the three at the early-
  tracking edge: "145 roles have appeared since, **confirmed on two consecutive
  nights**. What vanished, this copy cannot tell you: the build keeps no ledger
  of departures, and a count it does not hold is a count this page will not
  invent." B and C both print the unconfirmed number here; A alone enforces
  `first-seen.json`'s own note.

Held under 23 by felt costs the builder also names: under a stated standpoint
~90% of the sheet sits at 58% opacity and the first half-second reads as
*disabled, switched off* rather than *the board did not say* — the weakest
moment on the page, at the centre of its best idea. Rows are one fixed line, so
long titles clip ("Subject Matter Expert - Election Integrity, Violent
Extremism, an…"). Density never varies; the rhythm is all night-rules. The
depth gauge's "of 300 printed" went stale against a 2-result search, and the
same string sits untranslated in the ja edition (evaluator's to cap; noting the
feel).

## Originality — 18

The round's largest single invention: the Bound Volume is dead and the plate
survived it. Two printing plates — the register in black, identical for
everyone; the reader in magenta, laid **only where a fact exists** — is a
structural rule the page can be held to, and it holds it everywhere I looked:
the standpoint's rule, the row ticks, the folds, the cursor, the focus ring,
the wash, and a registration cross that appears in the corner only once a
second plate exists to register. A colour used once, meaning one thing, is what
the criteria ask for; this is that, system-wide.

The nine-select filter bank is replaced by three first-person statements, and
the page's epistemics are the best in the round: "**Reading is not
eligibility** … no board on this sheet states the language the work is done
in. **Remote is not borderless.** A board that says 'Remote — Germany' has
named a country, and it is still that country's job." The mostly-bare sheet
under a standpoint is presented as the finding, which it is. PRINT ONLY THE
OVERPRINT collapsing 6,423 to **543 postings from 47 companies that said so
themselves** is the most tellable true number the corpus has for a reader
outside the fifteen.

Not 19 because the sheet around the invention is still the atlas's austerity —
admirable, legible, cold — and the Japanese search bridge, while honest (script
census, "Show those 7", a disclaimed gloss chip), is the wordiest and most
buried of the three variants' bridges.

## For the next round

The overprint rule, the dealt sample, the fold-as-departure-ledger, and the
confirmed-only return count should survive in whatever wins. The dimmed-sheet
moment needs a third rendering state that reads as *silence* rather than
*disabled* — that is a design problem, not an honesty problem, and it is the
one thing between this page and the bar.
