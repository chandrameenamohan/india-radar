## The ask — 7

complete companies, nothing above it withheld — and the most honestly drawn:
a bar in the gauge's own scale, solid for what you have read, hatched for the
"4,590 roles below this line," with the round's best closing line ("the count
is the only evidence that would ever justify moving the register behind a
login, and it has not been earned"). It follows the reader's filter — searching

The 7 is structural: **the seam is 75,000 pixels down.** The free portion is
the twelve largest companies under canonical order, so the ask is met only by a
reader who has already descended through Databricks, Celonis and their kin —
most readers will never see it, which means the measurement the gate exists to
take will collect almost nothing, and the first hour of a first visit is
megacorp strata regardless of who the reader is. Generosity this extreme stops
being an ask and becomes a footnote.

## Originality — 9

An invented unit (depth in roles), an invented instrument (gauge, lens, needle,
horizons, seam), and motion that is all semantics: velocity fades the register
to what is legible at speed, the country bar's settle is the one animated
layout and it means "you are inside this now," and reduced-motion is treated
as a second designed state rather than a kill switch. The metaphor survives
contact with the full corpus — the seam, the strata, the fold are all the same
idea at three scales. It shares B's score but not B's warmth: C is original
the way a beautiful seismograph is original. Nothing on it knows the reader is
a person looking for a job at 11pm.
