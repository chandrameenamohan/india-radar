## B wins again, and by less than it should have

All three closed all four gaps, verifiably, as a reader — the profile is
stated never guessed in all three, all three keep something, all three greet a
return, all three cursors follow the eye (pressed `j` at depth in each; none
warped). That is the round doing what it was told. B wins on the same ground
it won round 1: it is the one a person at 11pm would stay inside, and the one
whose ideas compose instead of accumulating.

Three things decided it:

1. **B made the shortlist a word in its sentence.** "Show me *the 2 roles I
   kept* matching …" — gap 3 dissolved into the page's one organizing idea,
   with keep-bands by the nights you kept them. A's folds and C's notches are
   good; B's keeps are *of the page* rather than on it. And the ask writes the
   sentence around your count — "you have kept 2 roles, and they are in this
   browser alone. An account is the only thing here that could carry them to
   your phone" — the round's best answer to "what does yes buy".

2. **The reader-sentence.** "I am reading from *somewhere I have not said*"
   is the only profile UI of the three whose empty state speaks its own
   silence, and the lens it drives is opt-in, three-state, and prints its
   yield before asking to be believed (dims 209 · lights 543 · 5,671 exactly
   as bright as they were). The lane assignment — per-locale grammar — is
   real: the Japanese sentence is SOV with the typed word inside it; the
   German declines. The metaphor now reaches the reader, the keeps, the diff,
   the empty state, and three syntaxes.

3. **The Japanese dead end is best repaired in B.** デザイナー → "our words
   are in your language, and each is already a filter: **デザイン — 147件**."
   A bridge built from the only words the page owns. (A's script census +
   disclaimed gloss is honest but buried; C's `designer → 107` chip is the
   most direct and nearly as good.)

**B's one real error:** the return greet counts unconfirmed sightings. With
last-read 07-28 it says "6,423 roles appeared" — the whole register — because
tracking began 07-29. Its own footnote states first-seen's rule ("only
confirmed may be called new") and its NEW badge obeys it; the greet does not.
A alone got this exactly right: "**145** … confirmed on two consecutive
nights. What vanished, this copy cannot tell you … a count it does not hold is
a count this page will not invent." C prints the same overclaim as a bare
`+6,423` chip. Next round: A's clause, verbatim, wherever a diff is shown.

## The best of the losers, stated precisely

**From A — four things to steal whole:**
- *The overprint rule as a system*: one colour, laid only where a fact exists —
  reader's ticks, folds, cursor, focus, and a registration cross that appears
  only when a second plate exists. The mostly-bare sheet under a standpoint
  presented as the finding.
- *The three-door ask*: SIGN IN · **PRINT THE REST ANYWAY** · NO THANK YOU,
  all counted (`printed anyway` is its own ledger line). Bypass as a labelled
  door is the round's strongest trust gesture.
- *The fold as the only honest departure ledger*: the build records no
  departures, but a folded URL that left the corpus prints struck through —
  the one place the register can truthfully say "gone", found.
- *The epistemic margin notes*: "Reading is not eligibility … Remote is not
  borderless. A board that says 'Remote — Germany' has named a country." And
  the one most tellable number of the round: state "a country not surveyed
  here", press PRINT ONLY THE OVERPRINT, get **543 postings from 47 companies
  that said so themselves**.

**From C — two controls and a zero:**
- *Distance, derived and marked ≈*, with **nearest-first** ordering: state
  where you can work and the register becomes a staircase. The best wordless
  answer to "what could I do" in the round.
- *`designer → 107`*: translate the reader's word toward the boards, never a
  board's word toward the reader.
- *`↗ 0`*: the corrected brief as a single figure. It needs one sentence of
  companion or it reads as a broken number — but the instinct is right.

**Settled, as asked:** C's 4,662 is wrong. Its own rows double-mark 119 roles
(83 remote∧open, 36 remote∧closed; its ⊠ 209 is already 213 closed minus the
4 that also say open), so its subtraction-assuming-disjointness undercounts
silence; the true no-mark count under C's own rendering is **4,781** — the
corpus reading. Its footnote's "93% carry no mark" conflates visa-silence
(5,974∕6,423) with mark-silence (74%). One wound, but at the heart of the
text-light thesis: on that page the numbers are the prose, and two of them
lie. Also noted: A counts 10 non-Latin titles, C counts 11 — the Han-only
classification edge, named by both builders, dishonest in neither.

## What none of them tried

1. **Nothing lets the reader act.** All three end at "Open the posting ↗".
   No variant lets a reader state *applied* — a reader-stated fact, doctrine-
   clean, one bit in localStorage — so the keeps are a shelf, never a
   workbench, and the second week has no story: the shortlist cannot change
   state, so returning to it teaches nothing. The loved-website move past
   collecting is *progress*, and the product this register serves (getting a
   person referred into a company) lives exactly one step past this page's
   last control. Round 3's brief should be: **the register knows what the
   reader did about it** — kept → applied → answered, stated never inferred,
   burnable like everything else.

2. **All three go dark together the morning the build moves.** At 15:16 today
   the nightly loop rewrote `data/companies.json` (schema 10 → 11, 371 → 789
   companies, 6,423 → 27,687 roles — `design/iterations/data` is a symlink to
   live `data/`). All three variants refused to render it, each in its own
   voice, B's the best (its headline numbers become em-dashes; "fields may
   have moved"). The refusal is correct — a renderer must not guess at a
   contract it does not hold — and its uniformity is the tell: it is
   inherited convention, and **no variant designed the morning after.** None
   keeps the last-good sheet it printed (the keeps survive in localStorage;
   the register they point into does not), so the 11pm reader who comes back
   after a schema bump meets an apology instead of yesterday's copy marked
   *yesterday's*. A page that diffs itself nightly must expect to be stale
   nightly.

3. **Nobody designed visit seven.** All three greet the *second* visit with
   one diff banner; none accretes. A counts `ra2.visits` and changes nothing
   with it. No nights-read strip, no "your folds' history", no sense that the
   register and the reader are now old acquaintances. Habit is built from
   accretion, and the material (visit dates, fold dates, diff sizes) is
   already in localStorage in all three.

4. Smaller shared tells, for the record: all three hand-roll a private ja→en
   lexicon (12–24 concepts) for the bridge — the real fix is a bilingual
   occupation vocabulary in the *build*, which no renderer can reach; the
   `asked · signed in · declined` counter is now a convention rather than an
   idea, in identical form everywhere; and all three asks make the same offer
   (account = carry keeps across devices) in three phrasings, none of which
   the current Worker can honour — A says so, B says *could*, C says *would*.

## Round over round — the honest answer

The mean moved 57.5 → 62.5. The winner moved 63 → 64.5. **The winner's gain
is one and a half points, and most of the round's motion was the floor rising
to meet the old ceiling.** The four gaps were closed because they were named;
closing named gaps is what this harness now does reliably, and it converged
all three variants on the same honesty mechanics (stated profiles, printed
counters, bookplates, refusal prose) to the point where whole paragraphs are
interchangeable between them.

Would any of the three make a job seeker tell a friend unprompted? A's 543
number is the first artifact in two rounds I can imagine being sent to
someone ("look — these 543 said they'd take people from anywhere"). B is the
first page I stayed in past the testing. That is real movement, and it is not
arrival.

My read: **one more round, aimed only at the gaps above, then stop.** Round 3
should not re-brief the four gaps — they are closed, and re-polishing them
will produce decimal points. If round 3's winner does not clear ~68 on this
scale — does not produce the thing a reader *does something with* — the
harness has found this page's ceiling and the remaining distance belongs to
the product (the referral loop, the Worker, the build's vocabulary), not to
another lap of the renderer.
