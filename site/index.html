Every company here showed at least one open role, in one of the countries this radar covers, on its own job board on the snapshot date. A company we could not check is excluded rather than shown as "not hiring".

The visa and hiring-from-abroad badges are read from what a posting says, and most postings say nothing at all — 92% of the ones we measured. A role with no badge is one whose posting did not say, which is not the same as a no; where a posting does say no, the role says so.

The MCA data we hold lists companies registered in India as subsidiaries of a parent incorporated abroad. An India-founded company cannot appear in it at all, so a missing CIN says nothing about a company — and a present one is a registration, not an endorsement.

The plates and their coordinates are this page's own apparatus. Every marker sits at one fixed anchor point per country — the capital, except India's, which is Bengaluru — so a coordinate here places a country's marker, never a company's office. Each small dot is one listed company, drawn at its country's anchor. The longitude scale is not constant — it tightens where the plate is empty, 30°E to 90°E most of all — and the graticule prints each change of pitch, so the band spends its paper where the register is.
